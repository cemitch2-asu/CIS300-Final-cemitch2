Welcome to PHX On Air, a place to explore the sounds and artists connected to Phoenix. Find a new scene to follow, meet featured musicians, and take a look around the gallery.

Our Purpose

PHX On Air makes interacting with the scene easy! We bring breif guides to some of PHX largest scenes, artist highlights, and photography together to celebrate the music found in and around Phoenix.

What We Offer

  • Scene guidesExplore Indie, Alternative, and RnB.
  • Artist highlightsMeet musicians featured on PHX On Air.
  • Photo galleryBrowse images from the artists and scenes.

About Us

PHX On Air is a music discovery project built by Carl Mitchell. For the past 2 years, he has been an active part of Arizonas Local scene and has been documenting the music and artists he encounters. This site is a place to share his work and provide a resource for anyone curious about the city’s music.
